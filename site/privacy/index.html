Privacy

This site is built to process as little personal data as possible: no cookies, no analytics, no tracking, no storage on your device, and no requests to third-party servers from your browser. Version 1.0, 24 October 2026.

Email office@sintesismacro.com, telephone +49 152 22689578. No data protection officer has been appointed, because the statutory thresholds (§ 38 BDSG) are not met.

02 Hosting on GitHub Pages

This site is a set of static files published with GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., United States. GitHub states that when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor's IP address is logged and stored for security purposes, whether or not the visitor is signed in to GitHub. That processing is carried out by GitHub under the GitHub General Privacy Statement, which also describes the safeguards GitHub applies when data is transferred to the United States. SINTESIS has no access to these logs and receives no data about visitors. Legal basis for publishing this site in this way: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR; the legitimate interest is publishing the Forward Evidence Ledger and its tools securely and at no cost to visitors.

03 What your browser does here

Typefaces, images and data are delivered from this site itself; the page makes no further network requests after it has loaded. The calibration trainer computes in your browser: your probabilities and your score stay in the open page and are lost when you close or reload it. They are not stored on your device and not sent to SINTESIS. The site sets no cookies and uses no local storage.

04 Links

Links lead to GitHub repositories and to the publishers of the sources cited. When you follow one, the operator of that site processes your data under its own privacy policy.

05 Email

If you write to office@sintesismacro.com, your message is received and stored in Microsoft 365 (Exchange Online), which SINTESIS obtains from GoDaddy.com, LLC, with Microsoft as its sub-processor; the data location of the SINTESIS tenant is Germany. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR where your message concerns a possible engagement, otherwise Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in answering it. Messages are deleted no later than six months after the last contact unless an engagement follows.

06 Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18) and data portability (Art. 20). Requests: office@sintesismacro.com. You may lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR); the authority responsible for SINTESIS is the Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit, Alt-Moabit 59–61, 10555 Berlin.

07 Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR)

Where processing rests on Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, you may object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation. An informal email to office@sintesismacro.com is sufficient.

08 Automated decisions and changes

SINTESIS makes no automated decisions within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR. This notice is versioned and dated; earlier versions are available on request.
