01 — The Ledger

The record, scored in public

One file per forecast, with its question, source, resolution rule, date and probability. Continuous integration scores every resolved forecast and redraws this card. Nothing is deleted; a changed view is a new file.

  1. Step 1DraftedAgents draft the question, sources and both voices. The probability is left empty.
  2. Step 2OwnedOnly the named forecaster writes the probability, in a commit of his own.
  3. Step 3ScoredOn resolution, the Brier score against a permanent 50% baseline.
  4. Step 4TimestampedEach commitment receives a qualified electronic timestamp once the trust service is connected.

02 — The Atlas

The physical body of intelligence

Ten layers in three phases. Each layer will carry two scores from the Ledger's open forecasts: Becoming, the case that the build-out keeps extending, and Returning, the case that necessity is removed and the build-out exhausts itself. Select a layer to see its indicators, its practice questions and the open-source projects that build it.

03 — Calibration Trainer

How good is your judgement?

Fourteen questions that were genuinely open when they were asked and have since resolved, each checked against its primary source. Give a probability, commit, then see what happened. Your answers stay in this page: nothing is stored or sent.

04 — The Collection

Collected by judgement, not by fame

Open-source projects across the ten layers, each opened and checked by hand. Community, academic, foundation and public-research projects carry a reading; company-owned projects carry facts only. Propose a project on GitHub.

05 — The Canon

The thinkers behind the rules

Every rule of the Ledger is borrowed from someone who thought harder about it first. Select a thinker to see the rule it shaped.

06 — The Guild

Seats are earned by calibration

The Guild collects people by the quality of their forecasts, not their fame. Members forecast the same questions under the same rules, under a pseudonymous handle they choose, and appear here once they have ten resolved forecasts. The Arena opens with the Ledger's first questions; contributor terms are published before it opens.
